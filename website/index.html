Requirements and formats

Mac requirements
Apple silicon and macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Apple Intelligence needs a supported Mac, macOS 26 or later, and Apple Intelligence enabled.
Local and cloud AI
Transcription and Apple Intelligence run on your Mac. Optional cloud cleanup and suggestions send transcript text and any context you provide directly to your chosen provider.
API access
OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini require your own API key. Provider usage is billed separately from consumer chat subscriptions. No cloud account is needed for clipping, conversion, or local transcription.
Transcripts and captions
Transcription currently supports English. Export Text, Markdown, CSV, JSON, SRT, or WebVTT. Cleanup preserves timing; review corrections before using them.
Video and audio
Export MP4, MOV, MKV, or WebM video, or MP3, M4A, or WAV audio. Playback and Fast export depend on the source codec. See export options.
