## Choose a clip

1. Move the playhead to the beginning and press `I` to set the In point.
2. Move to the end and press `O` to set the Out point.
3. Drag either edge of the selection in the timeline for a visual adjustment.

> **Note:** Press `X` to clear the selection. Press `⌘` `A` to select the complete video.

## Playback and zoom

- Press `Space` to play or pause. Use `J`, `K`, and `L` for shuttle playback.
- Press `←` or `→` for precise, one-frame adjustments.
- Hold `⇧` with `←` or `→` to move 10 frames at a time.
- Hold `⌥` and `⇧` with `←` or `→` to move 100 frames at a time.
- Use `⌃` `Space` to play only the current In/Out selection.
- Use the zoom controls or keyboard zoom shortcuts to inspect detail or fit the complete timeline.
- Drag the divider below the player to resize it. Double-click the divider to toggle its preferred and maximum heights.

## Markers and frame capture

- Press `M` to add a marker at the playhead.
- Use `↑` and `↓` to visit In/Out points, regular markers, AI suggestion markers, and the boundaries of suggested ranges.
- Press `Delete` or `Backspace` to remove the selected regular marker.
- Choose Capture Frame to save a PNG image of the frame at the playhead.
