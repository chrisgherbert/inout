# What’s New

## More Accurate Audio Meters

- Live peak and RMS meters now measure the audio at its original sample rate, preserving the full audible frequency range.
- Improved level accuracy across different sample rates and audio formats, including clipping detection.

## Audio-Only Downloads

- SoundCloud and other audio-only sources now work with the compatible download presets.
- Compatible presets continue to prefer video when a supported video format is available.
