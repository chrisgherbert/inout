# What’s New

## Audio Level Monitoring

- Show optional stereo audio meters beside the video player while reviewing a clip.
- Click the meters to see peak and RMS levels, reset peak hold, and check for clipping.
- Analyze the current In/Out selection for integrated loudness, loudness range, and true peak.

## Playback and Reliability

- Audio metering now runs independently of playback to avoid delaying video startup.
- Fixed a crash that could occur when seeking rapidly while the meter prepared another part of the video.
