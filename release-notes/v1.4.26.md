# What’s New

## Aspect Ratio Framing

- Convert complete videos or Advanced clips to 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, or 4:5.
- Fit the complete picture, crop to fill, or place it over a blurred background.
- Drag the preview to position a custom crop, or choose a preset crop position.

## Faster Captions and Analysis

- Burned captions and profanity analysis now use the faster Parakeet transcription engine.
- Existing Parakeet transcripts are reused for captioned exports when possible.

## Other Improvements

- Timeline navigation buttons now match the Up and Down arrow shortcuts, moving between markers, suggestions, and clip edges.
