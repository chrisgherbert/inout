# What’s New

## Transcript Cleanup

- Clean up names, places, and recognition errors with Apple Intelligence or your preferred cloud AI provider while preserving caption timing.
- Review individual corrections in the transcript sidebar, apply them all, or use Quick Clean Up to accept corrections automatically. Hold Option for the toolbar shortcut.
- Keep working while cleanup runs, with progress and cancellation available in the activity area.
- Undo cleanup or restore the original transcript, including after reopening a saved transcript.

## A More Compact Transcript Sidebar

- Search, cleanup, view, and export controls share a compact toolbar, with cleanup options and results in a persistent popover.
- Search navigation appears inside the search field, and cleanup status appears below the transcript.
- Fixed corrected text failing to refresh in the sidebar and reduced redundant transcript updates.

## Updated AI Model Selection

- Refresh available models from your provider, with OpenAI models organized automatically by family and version.
