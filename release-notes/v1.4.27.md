# What’s New

## Safer Fast Exports

- Fast exports now expand to safe frame boundaries when necessary, ensuring the selected content is fully included.
- Improved Fast export reliability with long-GOP video files.

## Faster Export Workflows

- Hold Option to Quick Export transcripts and complete video or audio conversions without opening a save dialog.
- The current playhead timecode can now be clicked to copy it.

## Clip Control Improvements

- The selected clip duration is now shown beside the In and Out points.
- Refined timeline zoom, navigation, and player control layouts.
